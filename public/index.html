Our Story

Brktz started as a small project for fans who wanted a fun way to decide their favourites — from K-Pop idols to anime characters. The concept is simple: two options appear, you pick your favourite, and the winner advances. The last one standing is your personal champion.

How It Works

Every quiz is a single-elimination tournament where you decide who wins — based purely on personal preference. There are no right or wrong answers!

  • Round of 32 / 16 / 8 / 4 — Choose your tournament size
  • Two options shown at a time — Pick the one you prefer
  • Winner advances, loser is eliminated — until one champion remains
  • See your full bracket — Review every matchup after you finish

Our Mission

We believe fandom should be fun. Our mission is to create the world's most engaging preference tournament platform, built by communities for communities. Every quiz is a chance to discover your true favourites and share them with others.

📜 Community Guidelines

Be Respectful

Treat all members of the Brktz community with respect. Personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory content of any kind will not be tolerated.

Create Original Content

Only upload quizzes using content you own or have the right to use. Do not violate copyright laws or use copyrighted material without permission.

Keep It Appropriate

Brktz is for everyone. Do not post explicit, adult, or NSFW content. Keep quizzes appropriate for a general audience unless specifically marked for mature audiences.

No Spam or Misleading Content

Do not spam the platform with low-quality or misleading quizzes. Quiz titles and descriptions should accurately represent the content of the quiz.

Report Violations

If you see content that violates these guidelines, please use the report button or contact us directly. We review all reports and take appropriate action.

Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent banning from the platform, depending on the severity of the violation.
